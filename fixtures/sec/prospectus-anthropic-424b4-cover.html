This is an initial public offering of shares of Class A common stock of Anthropic, PBC. We are selling 956,351,354 shares of Class A common stock and the selling
stockholders identified in this prospectus are selling an additional 23,648,646 shares of Class A common stock. We will not receive any proceeds from the sale of shares
of our Class A common stock by any of the selling stockholders.
Prior to this offering, there has been no public market for our Class A common stock. The initial public offering price per share is $83.00. We have been approved to list
our Class A common stock on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol “ANT.”
