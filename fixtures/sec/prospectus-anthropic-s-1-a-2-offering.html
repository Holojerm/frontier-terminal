Use of proceeds
We estimate that the net proceeds from the sale of shares of
our Class A common stock by us in this offering and the
sale of shares of our Class N common stock by us in the
concurrent private placement will be approximately $84.6
billion (or approximately $93.7 billion if the underwriters’
option to purchase additional shares of our Class A
common stock is exercised in full), based upon the assumed
initial public offering price of $74.00 per share, which is
the midpoint of the estimated offering price range set forth
on the cover page of this prospectus, and after deducting
estimated underwriting discounts and commissions (which
apply only to the shares of Class A common stock sold in
this offering and not the shares of our Class N common
stock sold in the concurrent private placement) and
estimated offering expenses payable by us. We will not
receive any proceeds from the sale of shares of Class A
common stock by the selling stockholders.
The principal purposes of this offering and the concurrent
private placement are to increase our capitalization and
financial flexibility, create a public market for our Class A
common stock, facilitate an orderly distribution of shares
for the selling stockholders, and enable access to the public
equity markets for us and our stockholders. We intend to
use the net proceeds from this offering and the concurrent
private placement for general corporate purposes,
including working capital, operating expenses, and capital
expenditures, including investments in compute and
infrastructure, as well as hiring and retention of highly
skilled personnel. Additionally, we may use a portion of the
net proceeds to acquire or invest in businesses, products,
services, or technologies. However, we do not have
agreements or commitments for any material acquisitions
or investments at this time. We also intend to use a portion
of the net proceeds to satisfy our anticipated tax
withholding and remittance obligations related to the
settlement of certain of our outstanding RSUs. See the
section titled “Use of Proceeds” for more information.

Proposed purchases by certain existing
stockholders
Certain members of our board of directors and entities
affiliated or associated with them have indicated an interest
in purchasing shares of our Class A common stock in this
offering at the initial public offering price at an aggregate
purchase price of up to $303 million. Because these
indications of interest are not binding agreements or
commitments to purchase, the underwriters could
determine to sell more, fewer, or no shares to any of these
individuals or entities and these individuals or entities
could determine to purchase more, fewer, or no shares in
this offering. The underwriters will receive the same
underwriting discounts and commissions on shares of our
Class A common stock purchased by these individuals or
entities as they will from other shares of our Class A
common stock sold to the public in this offering. Any shares
purchased by these individuals or entities will be subject to
lock-up restrictions described in the section titled “Shares
Eligible for Future Sale.”
Market standoff and lock-up agreements
Our directors, officers, and the holders of substantially all
of our capital stock and securities convertible into or
exchangeable for our capital stock, representing an
aggregate of approximately 15.5 billion shares of our Class
A common stock (including shares issuable upon the
conversion of our outstanding Class N common stock, upon
the exercise or settlement of outstanding equity awards,
and upon full conversion of outstanding convertible
promissory notes), have entered into market standoff
agreements with us or have entered into lock-up
agreements with the underwriters that, subject to specified
exceptions, restrict transfers of their shares for up to 180
days or, in the case of our co-founders and certain of our
stockholders holding more than 0.5% of our fully diluted
shares prior to this offering, up to 365 days after the date of
this prospectus. Of these shares, 3.6 billion shares subject
to the 180-day lock-up and market standoff period may
become eligible for sale prior to the expiration of that
period, including up to 10% of the aggregate number of
lock-up securities held by our non-founder directors,
officers, and current and former employees and
consultants, which may be transferred or sold beginning on
the first trading day of our Class A common stock. See the
sections titled “Shares Eligible for Future Sale” and
“Underwriting.”
Voting rights
Shares of our Class A common stock are entitled to one vote
per share. Shares of our Class N common stock have no
voting rights, except as otherwise required by law. The
share of our Class T common stock has no voting rights
with respect to matters submitted to stockholders
generally, except as set forth within our amended and
restated certificate of incorporation, including with respect
to the election of a majority of our board of directors or as
required by law. The share of our Class F common stock
will be entitled to an aggregate number of votes that equals
50.1% of the total voting power of our outstanding shares of
capital stock as of the applicable record date on all matters
submitted to a vote of stockholders generally, including the
election of our directors other than the Class T directors.
Shares of our Class E common stock will be entitled to one
vote per share with respect to certain limited matters.
Controlled company
We are considered a “controlled company” within the
meaning of the Nasdaq Global Select Market corporate
governance requirements. See the sections titled “Our
Governance Structure and Its Goals,” “Management,” and
“Description of Capital Stock” for additional information.
